What happens when you delete your account?

Account deletion removes or anonymizes personal account information that is no longer required for legitimate business, legal, security or financial recordkeeping purposes.

Deletion may also remove your access to your profile, campaign activity, Ad Credit, advertising balances and other account features.

Information that may be retained

Some information may need to be retained for a limited period where required by law, necessary to prevent fraud or abuse, required for accounting or financial records, or necessary to resolve disputes and enforce applicable agreements.

Retained information will not be kept longer than reasonably necessary for the applicable purpose.
