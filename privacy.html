Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what information WhatsMart collects, how it is used, and the choices available to users.

Last updated: September 30, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use WhatsMart, we may collect information such as your name, city, state, WhatsApp number, account credentials, business information and other information you provide during registration or onboarding.

We may also collect information relating to your WhatsMart capabilities, campaign participation, advertising activity, Ad Credit, earnings, payout requests and account settings.

2. Campaign and Verification Information

When you participate in campaigns, WhatsMart may process campaign assignments, campaign status, verified-view information and related evidence.

Status Partners may be required to submit evidence associated with campaign verification. Such evidence may be stored securely and accessed only for purposes related to verification, fraud prevention, administration and platform operations.

3. Payment and Financial Information

If you purchase advertising credit or Ad Credit, request a payout, or participate in another financial transaction, WhatsMart may process information needed to record and verify the transaction.

Depending on the payment process used, this may include information such as sender name, bank name, transaction reference, payment reference, requested amount and related transaction records.

WhatsMart does not need your banking password, PIN or card PIN to operate its payment verification process.

4. How We Use Information

WhatsMart may use information to:

  • create and manage user accounts;
  • provide advertising and campaign services;
  • assign and verify campaigns;
  • calculate applicable balances, Ad Credit and earnings;
  • process payment confirmations and payout requests;
  • prevent fraud, abuse and manipulation;
  • maintain platform security;
  • provide customer support; and
  • improve and maintain WhatsMart.

5. WhatsApp Information

WhatsMart may collect a WhatsApp number when a user provides it for account or business-related purposes.

WhatsMart does not receive the private contents of a user's WhatsApp conversations merely because the user provides a WhatsApp number.

6. How We Share Information

WhatsMart does not sell users' personal information.

Information may be shared with service providers or infrastructure providers where necessary to operate the platform, such as hosting, database, storage, authentication, security or other technical services.

Information may also be disclosed where required by law, legal process, fraud prevention requirements, or to protect the rights, security and integrity of WhatsMart and its users.

7. Data Security

WhatsMart uses reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security, and users should also take reasonable steps to protect their account credentials and devices.

8. Data Retention

WhatsMart retains information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain legitimate business records, complete transactions, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, enforce agreements, or comply with legal obligations.

When information is no longer required for these purposes, it may be deleted or anonymized.

9. Account Deletion

Users can request deletion of their WhatsMart account through the account deletion functionality available inside the app.

Users who cannot access the in-app deletion feature can contact whatsmart2020@gmail.com to request assistance with account deletion.

Some information may be retained where required by law or reasonably necessary for legitimate financial, security, fraud-prevention or recordkeeping purposes.

For complete information about account deletion, visit the Account Deletion page .

10. Children's Privacy

WhatsMart is not intended for children who are below the minimum age required to use the service under applicable law.

We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children who are not eligible to use WhatsMart.

11. Third-Party Services

WhatsMart may rely on third-party infrastructure and service providers to operate parts of the platform. These providers may process information on behalf of WhatsMart as necessary to provide their services.

12. Your Choices

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights concerning access to, correction of, deletion of or other control over your personal information.

You can also request assistance by contacting whatsmart2020@gmail.com.

13. Changes to This Policy

WhatsMart may update this Privacy Policy as the service develops or as legal and operational requirements change.

The updated version will be published on this page together with a revised date.
